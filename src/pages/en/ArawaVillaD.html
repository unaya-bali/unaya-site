Villa D

Villa D sits at the head of the west row, on the access-road side. Its 105.41 m² plot is the larger of the two villas still for sale: same villa, more land around it.

Like villas E and F, it has access to a storage space, which the villas in the other row do not.

Inside, the Arawa layout: downstairs, a large living space with an open kitchen facing the private pool, a guest WC, a small cabana and an outdoor shower; upstairs, two bedrooms each with its own bathroom and walk-in wardrobe, plus a terrace. Fully furnished and equipped.

Structure and façades are finished, finishing works are under way (photos from 9 November 2026).

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner buy these villas?

Yes. The villa is sold leasehold: you have the right to use it for about 28 years, with a 30-year extension option. It is the most common route for foreign buyers in Bali. More on our Invest in Bali page.

Can I visit?

Yes. The villas are nearly finished, so you see the real property. Message us to book a time, or ask for a video tour.

Can the villa be rented out?

Yes, it can be a personal home or a rental. Unaya Management can run the rental for you: listings, guests, cleaning, maintenance and a monthly report. See our packages.

What is included in the price?

The finished villa, fully furnished and equipped, with a one-year warranty after handover. Ask us for the furniture list and the materials schedule.

Why two different prices?

Villa D sits on a larger plot (105.41 m² versus 91.80 m² for villa B).
