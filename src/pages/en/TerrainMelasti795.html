Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner take this land?

Yes. Through a lease (hak sewa) registered with a notary: you hold the right of use for 30 years, with a 30-year extension option. Freehold (SHM) is reserved for Indonesians or held through a PT PMA company. See our Invest in Bali page.

Can I take only part of it?

Yes, on lease, from 4 ares (400 m²). Freehold is only sold for the full 7.95-are plot.

What can be built?

In the pink zone (Zona Pariwisata): villas, hotels, resorts, boutique hotels, restaurants and cafés, with daily rentals allowed. Maximum site coverage 50%, floor-area ratio 2.0: on 795 m², up to about 397 m² of footprint and 1,590 m² of floor area.

How is the price calculated?

Lease: IDR 21,000,000 per are per year × number of ares × 30 years. Full plot: IDR 5,008,500,000 (≈ USD 275,800). Freehold: IDR 1,100,000,000 per are, i.e. IDR 8,745,000,000 (≈ USD 481,600). Dollars at the rate of 11 Nov 2026.

Can you build the villa?

Yes: Unaya handles design, the building permit (PBG) and construction, then renting it out.
