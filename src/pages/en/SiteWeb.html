Boost your income: what a website brings to your villa

“Don't underestimate the power of a website for your villa business. Whether it's attracting new guests or increasing revenue, a website can make all the difference.”

The benefits of a website for your villa

Airbnb and Booking.com remain essential, but they don't belong to you. Your own website works for you every day: it presents your villa the way you want, reassures guests and brings you direct bookings with no platform commission.

01

Increased visibility and brand awareness

A well-built website is found on Google by travellers looking for a villa in your area. It gives your villa an identity: a name, a style, photos shown at their best.

  • Google ranking on “villa + area” searches
  • Linked to your Google listing, Instagram and the platforms
  • A premium image that supports your rates
02

An improved guest experience

Before they even arrive, guests find everything they need: photos, amenities, directions, services and answers to common questions.

  • Photo gallery, map and practical information
  • Secure online booking and payment
  • Extra services: airport transfer, scooter, driver
  • Site in English and French, perfect on mobile
03

Cost-effective marketing

Your website is a tool you own: no platform subscription, no commission on every night. It becomes the destination for all your marketing.

  • Win back past guests: they rebook direct
  • Google or social media campaigns that point to your site
  • Special offers and promo codes you control
04

Increased revenue

Every direct booking saves you 15–20% in platform commission. Add service sales and repeat guests, and the site quickly pays for itself.

  • Direct bookings with no platform commission
  • Calendar synced with Airbnb and Booking.com, no double bookings
  • Upsells: transfers, scooters, extended stays
