Freehold or leasehold: what the law allows

In Indonesia, freehold land title (Hak Milik) is reserved for Indonesian citizens. Foreigners have three legal options.

Leasehold

You lease the land for a long period, often 25 to 30 years, with an extension negotiated from the start. Usually the most profitable route for renting.

Most common

A PT PMA company

An Indonesian company with foreign capital can hold a long-term, renewable right to build (Hak Guna Bangunan). Significant capital and rigorous accounting required.

To invest

Right of use

With a residence permit, a foreigner may, under conditions, hold a right of use (Hak Pakai) on a home to live in.

To live in

Avoid at all costs: nominee arrangements, where an Indonesian buys in their name on your behalf. They offer no protection and can cost you your property.

Tax numbers and taxes

NPWP

The national tax number. Automatic for a company; possible for a foreign individual under conditions, usually with a residence permit.

10%

Accommodation tax

On short-term rentals in Badung, paid by guests and passed on to the region. The villa must be registered.

Land and building tax (PBB)

Due every year on the land and the building.

Income tax

Rate and form depend on the structure (individual, company, dividends) and on tax treaties with your country.

Tax rules change regularly: this information is general and must be confirmed by an adviser.
