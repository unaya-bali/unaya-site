About

Unaya means “the unique one”.

A name that speaks of care, protection and a benevolent gaze. Because every home, every owner and every guest is one of a kind.

Our story

A boutique agency, born in Bali

Unaya Management looks after private villas in Uluwatu / Jimbaran, Tanah Lot / Canggu West and Seminyak / Kerobokan. Our choice is simple: manage fewer villas, but manage them closely. Every owner has one point of contact, every villa a team on the ground, every month a clear financial report.

We run our own villas to the same standard as those entrusted to us: bookings, guests, maintenance, accounting. That is why we can talk about them with precision.
