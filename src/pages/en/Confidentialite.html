Privacy policy

Data controller

PT Unaya Bali Grup (Unaya Management), Jalan Balangan Hijau VI No. 15, Jimbaran, Kuta Selatan, Badung, Bali — Indonesia. Contact: info@unayabali.com.

Data we collect

  • Website forms (contact, guest services, owners): name, email, WhatsApp or phone number, message and, depending on the form, details of your stay (dates, flight, number of guests) or of your villa.
  • Bookings: identity, contact details, dates and details of your stay, extras chosen, payment status.

How we use it

Your data is used only to answer your requests, manage your bookings and your stay (welcome, transfers, services), send you useful information and meet our legal and accounting obligations. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Service providers

We use providers who process some data on our behalf:

  • Beds24 — booking management
  • Midtrans — online payments
  • Cloudflare — website hosting
  • Resend — sending form emails
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — conversations when you message us or give us your number
  • Google — maps (Google Maps) and fonts shown on some pages
  • Airbnb, Booking.com and other platforms — when you book through them

Cookies

This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics tools. Embedded Google Maps and Google Fonts may collect technical data (such as your IP address) under Google's own rules.

Retention

Requests sent through our forms are kept for up to 3 years after our last exchange. Booking data is kept for as long as required by our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

In accordance with applicable laws, including Indonesia's Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its use, by writing to info@unayabali.com.
