The villa

White arches, pale wood and a tropical garden

Nestled between a golf course and the spectacular cliffs of Balangan in southern Bali, Villa Suma brings together five suites around a large, softly curved pool, framed by wooden decks and a lush garden. The beach is 5 minutes away, the airport 30 minutes.

White walls with rounded lines, natural wood, private open-sky bathrooms: every detail is designed for calm and the Balinese art of living. The bale, a traditional pavilion, invites you to nap or dine outdoors. Bali's finest beaches are 10 minutes away, renowned restaurants, bars and beach clubs 15 minutes, with surf spots, cliffs, temples and local warungs all around.
