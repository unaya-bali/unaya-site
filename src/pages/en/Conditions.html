Booking terms

Scope

These terms apply to bookings made directly with Unaya Management (PT Unaya Bali Grup), on this website or through our booking engine. Bookings made on Airbnb, Booking.com or other platforms are subject to that platform's terms.

Booking and payment

Your stay is paid in full (100%) at the time of booking, online and securely through our payment provider Midtrans (card, bank transfer, QRIS, e-wallets). Your booking is confirmed once payment is received, and a confirmation email is sent to you.

Prices are shown in Indonesian rupiah (IDR). The total amount, including extras (airport transfer, scooter…), is shown before you confirm your booking.

Cancellation by the guest

Direct bookings are non-refundable for all our villas (Villa Suma, Villa Yuga, Rumah Jambu): in case of cancellation, change of dates, no-show or early departure, the amount paid remains due and is not refunded.

We recommend taking out travel insurance that covers cancellation. For any question about your booking, write to info@unayabali.com.

Cancellation by Unaya

Should we have to cancel your stay (exceptional circumstances or force majeure), you will receive a full refund, and we will do our best to offer an equivalent alternative.

Guest services

Airport transfers, private drivers and scooter rentals are confirmed after payment. Their terms (times, changes, cancellation) are given when we confirm on WhatsApp.
