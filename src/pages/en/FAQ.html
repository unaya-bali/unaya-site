Your questions, answered.

Owners or guests, here are the essentials. Another question? Write to us, we reply within 24 hours.

Owners

How much does villa management cost?

Two plans: Full Management from 18%, Marketing Only at 11%. Commission is calculated on rental revenue after platform commissions and accommodation tax. No fixed fees.

What is the difference between the two plans?

With Full Management we handle everything: marketing, guests, staff, maintenance, expenses and accounting. With Marketing Only we list and optimise your villa on the platforms, manage pricing, online guest support and reviews; you keep running the villa on site.

Are photos included?

Photo, video and drone shoots are charged separately, paid by the owner, in both plans.

How do I get paid?

We collect the bookings, pay the villa's expenses and accommodation tax, then pay out your income every month.

When do I receive my report?

Every month you receive a detailed report: occupancy, income, platform commissions, expenses and management fees. You can also follow everything in real time in your owner portal.

Can I block dates to stay in my villa?

Of course. Let us know and we block the calendar on every platform at once.

Which areas do you cover?

Uluwatu / Jimbaran, Tanah Lot / Canggu West and Seminyak / Kerobokan for now.

Guests

How do I book?

On the villa's page, choose your dates: we reply quickly on WhatsApp with availability and price. Our villas are also on Airbnb and Booking.com.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3pm (2pm at Villa Yuga), check-out before 11am. A key box allows late arrivals at Suma and Yuga. For an early arrival, contact us.

What is the cancellation policy?

Direct bookings are paid in full and non-refundable; we recommend travel insurance. For bookings on Airbnb or Booking.com, the platform's policy applies. Details in our booking terms.

Do you arrange airport transfers?

Yes, from IDR 400,000 depending on the area. Book it on our Guest services page or when you book your stay.

Are pets allowed?

Yes at Rumah Jambu. Villas Suma and Yuga do not accept pets.

Can we throw a party?

No, parties and events are not allowed, and quiet is expected after 9pm, out of respect for the neighbours.
