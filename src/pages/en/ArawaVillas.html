The project

Arawa is a small residence of 6 villas in Balangan, on the Uluwatu side. Two are still for sale, B and D. Construction is 85–90% done: structure, façades and pools are in place, finishing works are under way.

Each villa has two bedrooms upstairs, each with its own bathroom and walk-in wardrobe, and a large living space opening onto the pool downstairs. It comes fully furnished and equipped. Live in it, rent it out, or both.

The Arawa philosophy

Arawa is about more than selling a villa. The project rests on four simple ideas.

Careful construction

Reinforced concrete, ironwood and teak selected in Java, furniture and joinery made to measure by Arawa.

Natural light

High ceilings and large glass doors: the house opens onto the pool and garden.

Tropical living that works

Spaces designed for everyday life: open kitchen, outdoor shower, cabana, walk-in wardrobe in each bedroom.

A transparent purchase

Regular progress updates and clear answers, from your first question to handover.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner buy these villas?

Yes. The villa is sold leasehold: you have the right to use it for about 28 years, with a 30-year extension option. It is the most common route for foreign buyers in Bali. More on our Invest in Bali page.

Can I visit?

Yes. The villas are nearly finished, so you see the real property. Message us to book a time, or ask for a video tour.

Can the villa be rented out?

Yes, it can be a personal home or a rental. Unaya Management can run the rental for you: listings, guests, cleaning, maintenance and a monthly report. See our packages.

What is included in the price?

The finished villa, fully furnished and equipped, with a one-year warranty after handover. Ask us for the furniture list and the materials schedule.

Why two different prices?

Villa D sits on a larger plot (105.41 m² versus 91.80 m² for villa B).
