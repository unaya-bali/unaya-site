Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner take this land?

Yes, through a lease (hak sewa) registered with a notary: you hold the right of use for 30 years, with a 30-year extension option. The Indonesian owner keeps the SHM title. You can also use a PT PMA company. See our Invest in Bali page.

What does “pink zone” mean?

It is the tourism zone of the spatial plan (Zona Pariwisata). It allows villas, hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafés and daily rentals, so the villa can be rented out legally.

How is the price calculated?

IDR 21,000,000 per are per year × 5 ares × 30 years = IDR 3,150,000,000. The dollar amount is indicative (rate of 14 October 2026).

Can you build the villa?

Yes: Unaya Build handles design, the building permit (PBG) and construction, then Unaya Management rents it out.
