Why Uluwatu and Jimbaran

This is the Bukit peninsula, at the very south of Bali: cliffs, white-sand beaches (Balangan, Bingin, Padang Padang, Melasti) and some of the best waves on the island. Guests come for the surf, the sunsets and the calm, far from Canggu’s traffic.

For an owner, it is an area that rents well all year round, with couples, families and surfers. Jimbaran, closer to the airport, also draws people with its bay and grilled-fish restaurants on the beach.

Frequently asked questions

How much does villa management cost in Uluwatu?

At Unaya, full management starts at 18% of income, calculated after platform commissions and accommodation tax, and marketing only is 11%. No fixed fees.

Do you also manage villas in Jimbaran, Ungasan or Pecatu?

Yes, the whole Bukit peninsula: Jimbaran, Balangan, Bingin, Padang Padang, Uluwatu, Pecatu, Ungasan and Melasti. Our office is in Jimbaran, so we are on site quickly.

Can I rent a villa by the room in Uluwatu?

Yes: Villa Suma, in Balangan, can be rented by the room or as a whole. Rumah Jambu is rented as a whole.

Can a foreigner buy a villa in Uluwatu?

Yes, on a leasehold (long lease signed before a notary). Our villas for sale in Balangan are leasehold. Our investment guide explains what to check.
