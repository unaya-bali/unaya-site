The villa

A tropical retreat among the rice fields

Welcome to Yuga, your tropical hideaway in Nyanyi, in the Tabanan regency. Surrounded by lush rice fields, the villa offers an intimate, peaceful stay, just 5 minutes from Nuanu Creative City and 10–15 minutes from Pererenan and Canggu.

Wake up to the rice fields, spend the afternoon by the pool and enjoy the perfect balance between quiet village life and Bali's most exciting new destination. An ideal setting for families and groups of up to 8.
