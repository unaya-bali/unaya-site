The villa

Yuga is four suites around one pool, on 310 m² of land facing the rice fields of Beraban. It sits on Jalan Pantai Nyanyi, the road down to Nyanyi Beach, in one of the fastest-moving corners of the Tanah Lot coast. Nuanu and its restaurants are 5 minutes away, Pererenan and Canggu 10–15 minutes, without their traffic.

Finished in 2026, it is sold fully furnished and equipped, and it already takes guests, who rate it 9.6/10. It rents as a whole or by the room, and the cleaning and pool staff are in place.

Since October 2026 it has been run by Unaya Villa Management. You can keep that management after you buy: nightly rentals carry on under Unaya’s licence, even if you live abroad.

Buying from abroad?

Since May 2026, a new PT PMA (foreign-owned company) can no longer get a licence in Bali to rent out a villa by the night. It is the main problem foreign buyers face right now.

With Yuga, that is solved: Unaya Villa Management can keep running the villa and renting it by the night under its own licence. You receive the income and a report every month, wherever you are.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner buy the villa?

Yes. It is sold as a leasehold: the land lease is transferred to you, with the villa, until 20 June 2054. It is the most common route in Bali for foreign buyers. More in our guide leasehold in Bali.

Can it keep renting by the night?

Yes. Unaya Villa Management already runs the villa and can carry on after the sale, under its licence. That matters because, since May 2026, a new PT PMA can no longer get that licence in Bali. See our management plans.

What does it rent for?

Today, from IDR 2,900,000 a night for the whole villa and from IDR 650,000 for one room (see the rental page). To estimate what a villa earns, read our step-by-step calculation.

What is included in the price?

The villa, its furniture and equipment (linen and guest supplies included), and the remaining lease years, until June 2054, with priority to extend.

Where are the permits?

The permits (PBG and SLF) are in progress: the application was filed in September 2026. We show you where it stands, and your lawyer can check it.

Is the price in rupiah or dollars?

The reference price is IDR 5,900,000,000. The dollar amount (≈ USD 325,000) is indicative, at the 11 November 2026 rate.
