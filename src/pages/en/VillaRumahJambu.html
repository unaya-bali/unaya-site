The villa

An authentic Balinese house in the heart of the village

A traditional Balinese house, recently renovated with modern comfort. Polished concrete, red brick and an alang-alang roof open onto a large garden of Balinese plants and trees, with its own temple bringing a peaceful energy.

In the heart of authentic Balangan village, live at the pace of the locals, minutes from Uluwatu's beaches, surf spots and beach clubs. Far from soulless villas, Rumah Jambu offers charm, comfort and a unique experience.
