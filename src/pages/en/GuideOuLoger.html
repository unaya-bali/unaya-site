Where to rent a villa in Bali: Uluwatu, Canggu, Seminyak or Tanah Lot?

By the Unaya team · Updated 16 November 2026 · 5 min read

Bali is bigger than you think, and traffic can turn 20 km into an hour’s drive. Choosing the right area matters as much as choosing the right villa. Here are the main areas to rent a villa, and who they suit.

In this article
  1. Uluwatu & Jimbaran: cliffs, surf and beaches
  2. Canggu: cafés, surf and neighbourhood life
  3. Tanah Lot & Nyanyi: rice fields and black sand
  4. Seminyak & Kerobokan: restaurants and beach clubs
  5. Our tips for choosing your villa

Uluwatu & Jimbaran: cliffs, surf and beaches

At the very south, on the Bukit peninsula. White-sand beaches between the cliffs (Balangan, Bingin, Padang Padang, Melasti), Uluwatu temple at sunset, and Jimbaran with its bay and seafood restaurants on the sand.

Who it suits: couples, families, surfers, and anyone wanting calm close to the airport (30–50 minutes). Our villas here: Villa Suma and Rumah Jambu, in Balangan.

Canggu: cafés, surf and neighbourhood life

The liveliest area on the west coast: cafés, coworking, restaurants, beach clubs and surf beaches. Great if you want to do everything on foot or by scooter, less so if you want silence.

Who it suits: young travellers, digital nomads, groups of friends.

Tanah Lot & Nyanyi: rice fields and black sand

Just west of Canggu, the coast turns to countryside: rice fields, black-sand beaches, and Tanah Lot temple on its rock. Canggu and Pererenan restaurants are 15–25 minutes away.

Who it suits: families, long stays, anyone after rice-field Bali. Our villa here: Villa Yuga, in Nyanyi, rented by the room or as a whole.

Seminyak & Kerobokan: restaurants and beach clubs

Seminyak and Petitenget are restaurants, boutiques and beach clubs by the sea. Kerobokan and Umalas, just behind, are quieter, with big family villas.

Who it suits: those who want nights out, shopping, and to stay 30–45 minutes from the airport.

Our tips for choosing your villa

  • Look at travel time between the villa and what you want to do, not just distance.
  • Check the villa is licensed: since 2026, unlicensed listings can be removed from platforms, sometimes after you have booked.
  • Book direct when you can: you talk to the team that welcomes you, and it is often cheaper (at Unaya, −15% from 7 nights).
  • Plan the airport transfer and a scooter or driver on site: in Bali you don’t get around on foot.
In short
  • Calm, beaches and surf near the airport: Uluwatu & Jimbaran.
  • Buzz and cafés: Canggu.
  • Rice fields and space: Tanah Lot & Nyanyi.
  • Restaurants and beach clubs: Seminyak.
